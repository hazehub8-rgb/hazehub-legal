These terms apply when using the Haze Hub Café food-ordering app in Tamghas, Gulmi.

01 Orders and delivery

Orders are for delivery within Tamghas valley. Enter a reachable phone number and a clear delivery place. The café may contact you to clarify details and may reject an order it cannot fulfill or deliver.

Prices are shown in Nepalese rupees. The delivery fee and minimum order are shown before checkout and may be updated by the café. There is no tax or online-payment feature in this app. Payment is Cash on Delivery when the order arrives.

Orders are accepted only while the café is accepting orders and during its published opening hours. The app displays availability, but the café may be unable to fulfill an item that becomes unavailable.

02 Cancellation and reviews

You may cancel in the app while an order is in Received status. Later cancellation or delivery issues should be discussed with the café. Ratings and comments should be honest, relevant, and respectful; Haze Hub may remove content that is unlawful or abusive.

03 Accounts and service availability

Sign-in uses Google. Keep your account secure and provide accurate delivery information. The service may be unavailable during maintenance, network interruptions, or café closures. Do not use the app unlawfully or interfere with its operation.
