Privacy Policy

How Haze Hub Café uses the information needed to prepare, deliver, and track your order.

Effective November 14, 2026 Haze Hub Café · Tamghas, Gulmi

In this policy

Haze Hub Café in Tamghas, Gulmi, Nepal (“Haze Hub”, “we”) uses information to operate food ordering and delivery within Tamghas valley.

01 Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. Your profile may also contain your phone number and the delivery place you choose to save. For each order, we process your name, phone, typed delivery place, selected food, prices, notes, payment method (Cash on Delivery), and order status. You may submit an optional food rating and comment. If you allow notifications, the app stores a device push token to send order updates.

The app does not request map/location access, collect GPS location, take online payments, or sell personal data.

02 How we use and share information

We use information to authenticate your account, prepare and deliver your order, contact you about the order, show order history, calculate food ratings, send status notifications, and protect or troubleshoot the service. Your phone and delivery place are shown to authorized café owner accounts for fulfillment.

Supabase hosts account and order data. Google provides sign-in. Expo Push Service and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver push notifications. Sentry may receive technical crash diagnostics when enabled. These providers process information for the service; they are not authorized by this policy to sell it.

03 Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion from Account → Delete my account in the app. The account and personal profile data are deleted; order records needed for café operations may remain in anonymized form. Push tokens are removed with the profile. For help, contact the café using the details below.

04 Security and children

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access is restricted by account permissions. No online service can guarantee absolute security. The app is not designed to collect information from children under 13.

We may update this policy when the service changes. Publish the revised policy and effective date before relying on the change.
