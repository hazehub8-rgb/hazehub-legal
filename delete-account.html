You can request account deletion in the app at any time by following the steps below.

01 Delete in the app

  1. Open Haze Hub and sign in with the Google account you used for ordering.
  2. Open Account, select Delete my account, and confirm.
  3. Keep the app open while it confirms deletion. The app signs you out when the request succeeds.

02 What is removed

Account deletion removes your profile and push tokens. Order history may be retained in anonymized form for café operations; it is no longer linked to your personal profile.

03 Need help?

If you cannot access the app, contact Haze Hub Café and request account deletion.
